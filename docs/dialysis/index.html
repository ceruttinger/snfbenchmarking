Provider Benchmarking

Dialysis Benchmarking

National benchmarking prototype built from the CMS Renal Dialysis Facility Cost Report (HCRIS CMS-265-11). This module extends the SNF benchmarking platform to freestanding renal dialysis facilities.

MVP pipeline under validation

Financial

Cost, revenue and margin measures, including normalized per-treatment benchmarks.

Utilization

Treatment volume and modality mix, including in-center and home dialysis.

Peer benchmarking

Facility performance compared with national and selected peer distributions.

Quality layer

Planned CCN-linked enrichment using CMS dialysis facility and quality data.

Data and methodology

CMS HCRIS contains provider characteristics, utilization, costs, charges, settlement and financial-statement information. Renal HCRIS is distributed as a multi-year file. The analytic pipeline retains worksheet/line/column provenance so every published KPI can be traced to Form CMS-265-11.

Current status: ingestion and metric architecture are in place. Worksheet mappings are being validated before financial or utilization values are displayed publicly.

Source: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. Derived analyses are not attributable to CMS or HCRIS.
