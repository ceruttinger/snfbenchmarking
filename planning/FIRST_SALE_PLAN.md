# First Sale Plan — SNF Benchmarking

## Objective

Generate revenue from the national SNF product before the full subscription platform is complete.

## Offer

Founding beta product:

**National SNF Benchmark Report**

Introductory target price: **$350–$500 per facility**.

Promise:

> A facility-specific financial, labor, occupancy, quality, and matched-peer benchmark showing where the SNF materially differs from comparable facilities and which differences deserve investigation first.

Do not promise guaranteed savings, reimbursement improvement, or clinical outcomes.

## Beta report requirements

A beta report is sellable when it includes:
- facility profile and ownership context
- transparent matched-peer definition
- 8–15 high-value financial/operating benchmarks
- three-year or longer trends where valid
- labor-cost section
- occupancy/utilization section
- quality/staffing context
- 5–10 prioritized findings
- source dates and methodology notes
- explicit data-quality limitations

## First buyer targets

Prioritize buyers who analyze multiple facilities:

1. SNF financial/operations consultants
2. regional/chain operators
3. lenders and transaction advisers
4. state associations
5. vendors that need facility-level intelligence

Secondary:
- individual administrators/CFOs

## Founding customer proposition

Founding customers receive:
- discounted beta report
- direct discussion of findings
- ability to suggest metrics/features
- credit toward a founding annual subscription if/when the subscription product launches

In exchange, request:
- candid usability feedback
- permission to use an anonymized testimonial or outcome statement where appropriate
- 20–30 minute feedback interview

## First 30 prospects

Build a prospect sheet with:
- contact
- organization
- role
- buyer type
- state/market
- number of facilities influenced
- relationship strength
- reason the product is relevant
- outreach status
- response
- demo/report facility

Avoid broad cold-email blasts at first. The goal is learning, not volume.

## Demonstration assets

Prepare three reports that demonstrate different facility archetypes:
- urban/high-cost facility
- rural/smaller facility
- multi-facility chain member or financially unusual facility

Use public data only and clearly label demonstration reports.

## Sales funnel v1

```text
public/free facility profile
        |
        v
see 3 benchmark findings
        |
        v
purchase full report / request example
        |
        v
$350–$500 facility report
        |
        v
subscription or portfolio upsell
```

## Validation questions

For each beta buyer, learn:
- Which finding would change what you investigate?
- Which metrics are interesting but not actionable?
- What information do you currently pay a consultant/data vendor for?
- Which comparison group do you trust?
- What would make this worth $1,000/year? $3,000/year? $6,000/year?
- Would you rather buy a one-off report or maintain access?
- Who else in your organization would use it?

## Revenue checkpoints

### Checkpoint 1
Sell 3 beta reports.

Target: $1,050–$1,500.

### Checkpoint 2
Sell 10 total reports or convert 5 report buyers to annual subscriptions.

### Checkpoint 3
Sell one portfolio/professional account.

## Expansion rule

Do not let adjacent HCRIS verticals delay the first SNF sale. Capture requests for HHA, Hospice, Hospital, FQHC, RHC, ESRD, and other provider types as product-demand evidence, but keep engineering focused on the SNF paid report until the first revenue checkpoint is reached.
