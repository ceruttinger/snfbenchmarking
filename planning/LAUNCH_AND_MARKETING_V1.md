# SNF Benchmarking — Launch and Marketing v1

## Goal

Generate the first paid SNF benchmark customers while the national platform is still being completed. Marketing should begin as soon as three strong national demonstration reports exist; do not wait for full subscription SaaS.

## Initial positioning

Primary message:

> Financial, labor, quality, and operating intelligence for every U.S. skilled nursing facility — benchmarked against facilities that actually look like yours.

Supporting promise:

> See where a facility materially differs from comparable SNFs, quantify the benchmark gaps, and identify what deserves investigation first.

Avoid positioning as:
- a generic CMS dashboard
- an AI chatbot
- guaranteed cost savings
- a replacement for accounting, reimbursement, clinical, or legal advice

## Launch offer

**Founding Benchmark Report**

Recommended first-five-customer price: **$395 per facility**.

After initial validation, test $495.

Founding customers receive:
- complete national benchmark report
- one feedback/review conversation
- credit of the report price toward a founding annual facility subscription purchased within a defined launch window

## Public website funnel

### Home page

Above the fold:
- what the product does
- facility search
- one visual example of matched-peer benchmarking
- CTA: `Find a facility`
- CTA: `Get the full benchmark report`

### Free facility page

Show enough value to establish credibility:
- identity / ownership / chain
- beds / urban-rural
- selected HCRIS trend
- selected quality/staffing measures
- 3 benchmark observations
- data/source freshness

Gate:
- full opportunity table
- detailed labor analysis
- full peer set
- report download
- expanded longitudinal analytics

### State pages

Examples:
- `/utah/skilled-nursing-facilities/`
- `/texas/skilled-nursing-facilities/`

Content:
- number of facilities
- state medians
- facility table
- links to facility profiles

### Chain pages

Where source data support a stable chain ID/name:
- portfolio profile
- facility list
- basic aggregate comparisons

These pages create useful search inventory and professional-user entry points.

## First three demonstration reports

Choose facilities that demonstrate different analytical stories:

1. urban facility with high labor/operating costs
2. smaller/rural facility where crude national comparisons would be misleading
3. chain-affiliated facility with strong peer/portfolio context

Selection should be based on data quality and narrative usefulness, not notoriety.

## Customer segments and message

### Operator / CFO

Message:
> See where your facility's economics differ from comparable SNFs before spending weeks building the benchmark yourself.

### Regional / chain leader

Message:
> Find the facilities in your portfolio that are true exceptions rather than reviewing every metric for every building.

### Consultant / accountant

Message:
> Generate a defensible facility benchmark quickly and spend your time interpreting it with the client.

### Lender / investor / broker

Message:
> Screen facility financial, staffing, quality, and peer context from one place before deeper diligence.

### Vendor / staffing company

Message:
> Identify facilities with patterns relevant to your service instead of prospecting from a generic provider list.

## Acquisition order

1. Warm/direct professional outreach
2. LinkedIn posts using interesting aggregate findings from the national dataset
3. Search-indexed facility/state pages
4. Association and consultant partnerships
5. Email/newsletter content
6. Small paid-search tests only after the conversion funnel is working

Do not begin with broad paid advertising. First prove that visitors who understand the product will buy the report.

## Content engine

The national dataset should produce marketing content automatically.

Examples:
- states with highest/lowest median reported SNF labor cost per resident day
- contract labor patterns where supported
- occupancy distribution by rural/urban status
- differences between state and matched-peer rankings
- changes in operating expense per day over time
- facilities/markets with largest year-over-year shifts, after data-quality screening

Each analysis can become:
- website article
- LinkedIn chart/post
- email newsletter item
- sales conversation asset

Never publicly shame an individual facility based on a noisy or questionable metric. Use data-quality filters and contextual wording.

## Outreach workflow

Maintain a prospect table with:
- buyer
- organization
- role
- segment
- facilities influenced
- relationship source
- demo facility
- date contacted
- response
- next step
- report purchased
- subscription potential

Target the first 30 prospects manually.

## Funnel metrics

Track from launch:
- facility searches
- free profile views
- report CTA clicks
- checkout starts
- completed purchases
- report downloads
- feedback interviews
- subscription conversions
- acquisition source

The first useful conversion benchmark is not thousands of visitors. It is whether qualified professional users who view a relevant facility page will pay for the deeper report.
